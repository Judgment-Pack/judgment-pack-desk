# Desk v0.13.1

Desk adopts the independently published official Policy draft
[`policy-v1.0.0-draft.1`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-spec/releases/tag/policy-v1.0.0-draft.1)
through its shared Runtime check boundary. Fresh adoption preparation and review
select format `1.0.0`, `draft` maturity, and
[`spec/policy.md` at `0799b37b74e35c4a86bfbf70f7bee80bdb7bbe94`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-spec/blob/0799b37b74e35c4a86bfbf70f7bee80bdb7bbe94/spec/policy.md)
with exact published capabilities and complete result validation.

Pre-publication candidate reports remain inspectable historical evidence under
their original exact contract. Legacy prototype `0.0.2` coverage remains
supported. Existing documents, approvals, Jobs, registrations and grants are
preserved without conversion, enrollment, migration or automatic replay. Fresh
preparation and approval require the official draft check. An unsupported or
outdated Runtime keeps saved selections/history available and identifies the
required Runtime support.

Offline draft checks bind exact supplied bytes, anchors and scoped references.
They do not establish semantic Policy correctness, IAM, current source authority
or admission permission. Existing manual admission, exact frozen release/runtime,
lifecycle, custody, signer and witness gates remain separate. Runner `v0.24.0`,
Gateway `v0.21.0` and harness `v0.1.0` are unchanged.

The one actionable Runtime message is available in English and all 11 existing
translated locales. Strict catalogue checks pass at 6,793 messages per locale;
these model-authored translations have not received native-speaker validation.

[Policy smoke checklist](../design/v0131-local-validation.md). The deferred

research quality remains unverified.
