# Desk v0.13.0

Desk connects reusable research recipes, reviewed records and Pack decisions in
one workflow. The Library brings Packs, Graphs, Dialects and Policies together;
shared chat and workspace controls support their authoring and review. Local
Policy management adds retained sources, review, immutable publication and
explicit lifecycle decisions.

## What changes

- **Library:** find saved items and independent drafts by type and folder, then
  reopen their workspace and related conversations. Folder organization does
  not introduce team workspaces or access roles.
- **Reusable Dialects and research:** author requested fields, source declarations
  and mappings, or import exact reusable Exchange templates. Review local
  connection bindings separately from the published definition. Retained results
  include source receipts, field provenance, limits and unavailable values;
  selected records can be reviewed and reprocessed without replacing their
  original source data. Unsupported or unbound sources are identified explicitly.
- **Reviewed data to Pack decisions:** inspect original and prepared values,
  confirm typed mappings and exact Pack references, and create test cases from
  reviewed records. Missing values, supplied nulls, zero, false and original
  types remain distinct. Saved tests and Runtime checks bind to the candidate
  actually checked; changing it invalidates that evidence.
- **Publications and Jobs:** review immutable artifact versions independently of
  saving a draft. Existing Jobs and active subscriptions retain their exact
  reviewed release; newer publications require an explicit reviewed upgrade.
  Patch, minor and major version choices do not determine compatibility or grant
  permission. Retained run references and explicit saved-record verification
  distinguish signatures, independently retained release/input references and
  the recorded decision.
- **Decision and Jobs records:** retain main's per-run signer checks against
  Desk's independent Runner key list and explicit export verification. The Jobs
  chain carries no signature sidecar of its own. An optional independently
  operated witness can countersign checkpoints; Desk retains its statements and
  Runtime reports their coverage. Neither operator-held records nor a colluding
  witness establish independence or the truth of the underlying decisions.
- **Selected company to person:** review company results, select companies, then
  explicitly start bounded public professional-person research. Retained results
  and receipts support review and recovery. This is not autonomous outreach or
  an external CRM write, universal LinkedIn access, proof of completeness or a
  guarantee of a current role or deliverable email address.
- **Shared AI work:** contextual chats, project memory, Plan, Ask me/Auto, request
  allowance, progress and error/recovery controls follow shared execution rules
  across supported API families. Existing artifact conversations retain test
  design and review context. Proposed, applied, saved and checked revisions stay
  distinct. Bounded correction preserves expected test outcomes and stops for
  clarification or review when necessary.
- **Shared workspace behavior:** consistent loading and notices, retained cached
  reads with freshness/error states, action controls, unsaved-change guards,
  navigation, test pages and record review. Failed refreshes preserve the last
  available snapshot rather than presenting it as a fresh empty result.
- **Local Policy lifecycle:** import and retain original documents and revisions,
  author drafts, review and approve exact candidates, publish immutable versions,
  and manage their lifecycle. Externally managed source observations, recurring
  access consent and status reconciliation stay separate from approval. New
  source content does not silently approve a revision or execute work.
- **Policy interpretation and recovery:** compare revisions, review exact
  coverage/adoption references, and start Pack or Graph authoring from retained
  Policy material. Policy recovery archives retain original material and history
  within their documented scope. Coverage checks do not prove completeness or
  correct interpretation; readable source and optional structured requirements
  support review, while the Pack or Graph defines executable judgment.

## Data, permissions and compatibility

These Policy features serve one local administrator. They add no team invitations,
live multiuser editing, delegated RBAC, electronic-signature standard or general
Policy compliance certification. The independently published
[Policy `policy-v1.0.0-draft.1`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-spec/releases/tag/policy-v1.0.0-draft.1)
is accepted at **draft maturity**, with exact format `1.0.0` and unchanged Core;
its official contract is
[`spec/policy.md` at `0799b37b74e35c4a86bfbf70f7bee80bdb7bbe94`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-spec/blob/0799b37b74e35c4a86bfbf70f7bee80bdb7bbe94/spec/policy.md).
That publication does not adopt the contract in software. Desk `v0.13.0` and
Runtime `v0.36.0` deliberately retain earlier experimental prototype `0.0.2` /
candidate `1.0.0` Spec bytes, not the official schema identities or result
maturity. They make no official Policy conformance or portable execution-authority
claim. Official Runtime adoption is tracked separately in
[Runtime #293](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/issues/293).

Prompts, memory, saved checks, history and historical approval receipts cannot
grant a new write or execution permission. Plan, action approval and compute
allowance are separate controls. Restoring a conversation or observing a successful
check does not authorize a mutation. Interrupted or uncertain operations never automatically
replay. Research fixtures verify orchestration, typing and retained receipts;
they do not establish live source quality, factual accuracy or discovery reliability.

Before an upgrade or move, preserve the relevant private stores and their original
records, Runtime executables, signing-key history and independent trust references.
Jobs use the binaries and identities frozen by their release, not today's latest
version. Chat backups and Policy recovery archives have different scopes; neither
is a complete Jobs or project backup. A restored Policy history does not restore
current approval, source consent or execution authority. Current Policy archives
refuse execution-enabled schema-3 ledgers. Older Desk builds may reject newer
folder, draft or recovery metadata; downgrading does not convert or erase it.
See [Policy recovery](../design/policy-recovery-archive.md) and
[bundle/record verification](../release-verification.md).

## Optional experimental manual admission

The private `local-admin-manual-pack/1` profile requires explicit trusted
enrollment of an entire workspace and compatible companions. It supports only
immutable registered manual single-Pack jobs under the current local
administrator. It is not enabled by default or by a successful capability probe.
This release's component lock selects compatible published Runtime and Runner
versions. That selection does not enroll a workspace or grant execution authority.
Archive manual-admission checks and isolated default-off installed acceptance are
recorded below; upgrading does not silently migrate or enroll a workspace.

Registration, lifecycle decisions and each start use the same independently
anchored Policy authority. Admission binds the original Idempotency-Key and exact
raw request/facts/evidence to the immutable job/release and current eligibility.
The host holds external exclusive ownership continuously, including offline
preparation and actual Runtime children. Only the original fresh committed start
reply, durably journaled locally, can produce a launch consumed once. Lost
replies, recovered receipts, history and restart recovery never authorize a launch
or automatic replay. Withdrawal committed first blocks; a committed logical
start may physically spawn after a later withdrawal. This is not an atomic
ledger/OS transaction or protection against hostile code under the same OS user
or replacement/rollback of the entire trusted plane.

Enabled workspaces hold triggers, workflows, acquisition/provider operations,
mapped or prepared inputs, profile installation and Job upgrades, including
legacy resources. General Graph Jobs remain unsupported. Enrollment requires
explicit offline idle-store preparation and matching independent custody demands;
normal opening does not migrate enabled restored stores. Earlier companions
refuse the new execution state. There is no supported in-place downgrade or
automatic undo; a portable pre-enrollment backup alone cannot clear retained
external demands. Partial preparation or a lost reply requires reviewed
reconciliation. See [manual admission](../design/policy-manual-admission-host.md).
